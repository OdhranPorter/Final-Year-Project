## Summarization

Transform long study notes into concise, focused summaries using AI.

### How It Works

**Step 1: Enter Notes**
- Tap the **Text** button on the home screen
- Paste or type your study material
- Minimum 50 characters required

**Step 2: Generate Summary**
- Click **"GENERATE SUMMARY"** button
- Watch the spinner while AI processes
- Summary appears in a few seconds

**Step 3: Review & Save**
- Read the generated summary
- (Optional) Enter Class and Year for organization
- Click **"Save This Summary"**

### Tips for Best Results

✅ **Use complete sentences** - "Machine learning is a subset of artificial intelligence that enables systems to learn from data" works better than "ML = AI subset"

✅ **Include context** - Don't just list terms; provide definitions and relationships

✅ **Copy full paragraphs** - More content = better context for AI

✅ **Add section headers** - Helps AI understand content structure

❌ **Avoid incomplete text** - Fragments confuse the summarizer

❌ **Don't mix multiple topics** - Focus on one subject per summary

### Example Workflow

```
Original Notes:
"Photosynthesis is a vital biological process that allows green plants 
and other photosynthetic organisms to convert light energy into chemical 
energy stored in glucose. The process occurs in two main stages: the 
light-dependent reactions in the thylakoid membranes of chloroplasts, 
where light energy is captured and converted to ATP and NADPH, and the 
light-independent reactions (Calvin cycle) in the stroma, where CO2 is 
fixed into glucose..."

↓ [AI Processing]

Generated Summary:
"Photosynthesis is the process by which plants convert light energy into 
chemical energy (glucose). It has two stages:
1. Light-dependent reactions (thylakoids) - capture light energy → ATP/NADPH
2. Light-independent reactions/Calvin cycle (stroma) - fix CO2 → glucose"
```

## Quiz Generation

Test your knowledge with AI-generated questions based on your material.

### How It Works

**Step 1: Access Quiz**
From a saved summary or after generating one:
- Click the **"Quiz"** button
- System generates 5 auto-graded questions

**Step 2: Answer Questions**
- Read each question
- Select your answer from 4 options
- Progress indicator shows current question

**Step 3: Submit & Review**
- Click **"Submit"** after all answers
- View your score (e.g., 4/5 = 80%)
- Review answers with explanations

### Quiz Features

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Auto-grading** | Immediate feedback on answers |
| **Progress Tracking** | See how many questions completed |
| **Score History** | All quiz attempts saved |
| **Filtering** | Filter by subject or year |
| **Delete** | Remove unwanted quiz attempts |
| **Explanations** | Understand why answers are correct |

### Tips for Effective Learning

✅ **Take quizzes multiple times** - Different questions generated each time

✅ **Space them out** - Use spaced repetition (quiz same material after 1 day, 1 week, 1 month)

✅ **Review mistakes** - Learn from incorrect answers

✅ **Track progress** - Compare scores across sessions

### Example Quiz Session

```
Question 1 of 5: What is photosynthesis?
A) A disease in plants
B) The process by which plants convert light into chemical energy
C) A type of fungus
D) The breakdown of glucose for energy

Your Answer: B ✅ CORRECT

Question 2 of 5: The light reactions occur in which part of the chloroplast?
A) Stroma
B) Thylakoid membranes
C) Cell wall
D) Vacuole

Your Answer: A ❌ INCORRECT
Correct Answer: B (Thylakoid membranes)

[Final Score: 4/5 = 80%]
```

## Flashcard Creation

Build digital flashcards for spaced repetition learning.

### How It Works

**Step 1: Generate Flashcards**
From saved summary or live session:
- Click **"Flashcards"** button
- AI generates 10 flashcard pairs by default
- Each card has a question (front) and answer (back)

**Step 2: Save Flashcard Set**
- Review generated cards
- Enter a title (e.g., "Biology Ch. 5")
- Click **"Save"**

**Step 3: Study Your Cards**
- Go to **"Study Library"** → **"Flashcards"**
- Tap a flashcard set
- Cards appear one at a time
- Tap to flip and reveal answer
- Swipe left/right to navigate

### Study Features

| Feature | Benefit |
|---------|---------|
| **Flip Animation** | Engaging study experience |
| **Progress Counter** | See position in deck (3/10) |
| **Shuffle** | Study in random order |
| **Filter by Subject** | Focus on specific topics |
| **Delete Old Sets** | Clean up your library |

### Spaced Repetition Strategy

**Recommended Schedule:**
- 📅 Day 1: Learn (initial exposure)
- 📅 Day 3: Review (strengthen memory)
- 📅 Day 7: Review (long-term retention)
- 📅 Day 30: Final review (mastery)

### Example Flashcard Set

## Document Scanning (OCR)

Instantly convert physical documents into digital text.

### How It Works

**Step 1: Scan Document**
- Tap the **"Camera"** button on home screen
- Point at textbook, whiteboard, or printed sheet
- Ensure good lighting and clear text

**Step 2: AI Extracts Text**
- Google ML Kit processes image in real-time
- Extracted text appears in text field
- Auto-corrects common OCR errors

**Step 3: Use Extracted Text**
- Edit if needed
- Generate summary immediately
- Or copy to notes app

### Camera Tips

✅ **Good Lighting** - Natural light or bright room

✅ **Stable Position** - Hold phone steady for 2 seconds

✅ **Clear Text** - Use documents with readable fonts (12pt+)

✅ **Perpendicular Angle** - Point camera directly at text

✅ **Full Document** - Capture entire text area in frame

❌ **Avoid**
- ❌ Handwritten notes (ML Kit works better with printed text)
- ❌ Small fonts (<10pt)
- ❌ Blurry photos
- ❌ Heavy shadows or reflections
- ❌ Photos taken at angles

### Example OCR Workflow

```
[Camera captures textbook page]
         ↓
[ML Kit processes image]
         ↓
[Extracted Text]:
"The mitochondria is the powerhouse of the cell. It produces ATP 
through cellular respiration, converting glucose and oxygen into 
usable energy..."
         ↓
[Edit if needed] → [Generate Summary] → [Save]
```

## Troubleshooting

### "Summary generation failed"
- ✅ Check internet connection
- ✅ Ensure API is running
- ✅ Try with shorter notes

### "Quiz not generating"
- ✅ Verify text is substantial (>100 chars)
- ✅ Check internet connection
- ✅ Wait 30 seconds and retry

### "OCR not recognizing text"
- ✅ Improve lighting conditions
- ✅ Hold camera steady
- ✅ Use printed text (not handwritten)
- ✅ Try document scanning apps first

### "Filters not saving"
- ✅ Update to latest app version
- ✅ Ensure class/year fields filled
- ✅ Click Save before leaving
